1. The build was triggered by a `push` event to a tracked branch, not

2. The run was executed on a fresh `ubuntu-latest` GitHub-hosted

5. The fixture SHA-256 (`3b080b50...`) was re-verified at STEP 1 of

   Target Freeze artifact.
